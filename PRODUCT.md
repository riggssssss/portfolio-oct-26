# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), deploy target Vercel (assumed, not confirmed).

## Users

Primary: recruiters and design leads evaluating Adrián García (product designer) for an in-house role. They skim fast, open one or two case studies, and decide whether to reach out.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of a product designer. Success = a recruiter understands who Adrián is in seconds, opens a case study, and contacts him.

## Positioning

The portfolio is framed as a social feed: each case study is a "post" with a date, title, media and caption, opening into its own case-study page. The format itself demonstrates product thinking — the portfolio behaves like a product, not a brochure.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Case studies = posts in a feed; each post opens a detail page with the full case study.
- No comments, likes or reactions (removed 2026-10-20 at user request). Sharing a post link is the only social action.

## Brand Commitments

- Background `#FFFFFF`, darkest ink `#272724` (user-specified, binding; ground changed from #ECECEA to pure white #FFFFFF on 2026-10-20 at user request).
- Typeface: Figtree (Google Fonts via next/font), chosen 2026-10-20 after comparing Instrument Sans, Inter Tight, Urbanist, Open Sans and Coolvetica.
- Visual references given by user: copedesign.xyz, a minimal profile/feed layout (avatar, name + handle, one-line bio, chips like "Available for hire"), and a "Wayward"-style updates feed (dated posts, left profile column).

## Evidence on Hand

First real case study: **Mockp** (told by Adrián, 2026-10-20). A Pinterest-style gallery of free mockups for any user, with an integrated editor. Origin: while designing content, original/unique mockups meant either paying (and they were still overused) or building each one by hand in Photoshop, slow and single-use. The editor auto-detects the area to replace (it must be chroma green), respects masks, highlights and shadows, feels like a hand-made Photoshop result, and produces a ready mockup in ~10 seconds. Unconfirmed: role, duration, date, gallery size, live URL, real imagery.

The other three case studies are still invented examples. All their projects, images, metrics and dates are placeholders and must be visibly marked as such (TODO). Name "Adrián García" inferred from account; handle, bio, links unconfirmed.

## Product Principles

1. The feed is the portfolio: browsing should feel like scrolling a profile, not reading a CV.
2. Work first: the projects carry the page; chrome stays out of the way.
3. Recruiter speed: role, availability and contact reachable in one glance from anywhere.
